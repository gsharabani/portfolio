Why this plan?

Turning AI into a decision-making tool that helps employees understand not only which plans are recommended, but why one may be a better fit than the alternatives.

The challenge

The decision-support tool presented employees with several recommended plans, but understanding why one was a better fit than another wasn’t always easy. We saw an opportunity to use AI to explain the differences and help employees make more informed decisions.

01

Translating recommendation data into AI-powered explanations

I worked on shaping the AI prompt to translate complex plan data and personalized context into clear, relevant explanations. The challenge was to go beyond match scores and premiums, surfacing the factors that could genuinely influence a user’s decision.

02

Refining what tips the balance

Through iterative prompt development and unmoderated user testing, I explored what users understood, what was missing, and which trade-offs mattered most. The iterations refined how the AI explained a plan’s value, including factors such as coverage, access to care, and HSA eligibility when relevant.

03

Designing for clarity and trust

The feature combines an instant summary with an expanded, personalized explanation-giving users a quick understanding of why a plan may fit, with more detail available when needed. The AI was designed to communicate relevant benefits and trade-offs neutrally, without exposing internal scores or recommendation calculations.
